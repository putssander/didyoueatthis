didyoueatthis

Did a language model eat your document? Ask it to spit it back out.

Does a model reproduce this document verbatim? Passages are cut into a prefix and a hidden suffix; the models are asked to continue verbatim at several prefix lengths; exact reproduction that a matched control does not show is graded as memorisation evidence. This does not prove training-set membership, and no signal is not evidence of absence. Grading rules: docs/01-design.md.
